# 🌿 Eunola

> Designed to Celebrate Love

Eunola is a modern digital celebration platform that creates elegant invitation websites and meaningful digital experiences inspired by timeless design and Indonesian cultural heritage.

---

## Vision

To become Indonesia's most trusted digital celebration platform where beautiful design meets meaningful stories.
